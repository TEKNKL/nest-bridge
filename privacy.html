Privacy policy

Nest Bridge · Last updated November 13, 2026

Who uses this app

Nest Bridge is used only by its owner, to connect the owner's own Google Nest devices to the owner's own Apple Home. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's explicit permission through Google's consent screen, Nest Bridge uses the Google Smart Device Management API to read and control the Nest devices the owner chooses to share: device names and types, thermostat settings, camera and doorbell live streams, and device events such as motion or doorbell presses.

How the data is used

This data is used only to show and control those devices in the owner's Apple Home app. It is processed on a computer in the owner's home. Camera video is streamed live and is not recorded or stored by Nest Bridge.

Sharing

Nest Bridge does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising or analytics. Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

The only Google data stored is the sign-in token needed to stay connected, kept in a configuration file on the owner's home server, which is not reachable from the internet.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access page, or from the Nest partner connections settings. Removing access stops Nest Bridge from reaching the devices.
