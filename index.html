Nest Bridge

A private, personal-use home automation bridge.

Nest Bridge is a small self-hosted program that runs on a computer in its owner's home. It connects the owner's own Nest thermostat, cameras and doorbell to Apple Home, using Google's Smart Device Management API with the owner's permission.

It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else. There are no user accounts, no sign-ups and no data collection beyond what is needed to show the owner's own devices in their own Apple Home app.

Privacy policy

Nest Bridge is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Apple.
